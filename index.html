FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where can I download StashWhisper?

Exclusively through the Microsoft Store — we don't offer a direct .exe download. Installing, updating, and (once available) purchasing Pro all happen through the Store, which also handles license verification and Store-based refunds.

Is my audio uploaded to any server?

No. Recording and transcription are 100% local — your audio files and transcripts are processed and stored on your computer with whisper.cpp and are never uploaded automatically.

How do AI Study Notes actually work?

StashWhisper does not run its own note-generating AI. Instead, one click exports a ready-made prompt — your transcript, pre-formatted — as a .txt file. You upload that file to the AI chat service you already use (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any other), copy its response, and paste it back into StashWhisper's "AI Study Notes" tab to save it with your session.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 or 11 with at least 8 GB of RAM. StashWhisper checks available memory on launch and during setup — below roughly 7.5 GB of usable RAM, the app can still be installed, but live transcription stays disabled until you're on a machine that meets the requirement.

How does the free trial work?

You can use the full StashWhisper workflow for 3 complete sessions before purchasing a Pro license.

Can I use StashWhisper without an internet connection?

Yes, for recording and transcription — both run entirely offline. An internet connection is only needed for the optional step of uploading an exported prompt to an AI chat service to generate study notes.

How do I activate my Pro license?

Click "Upgrade to Pro" in the app and choose "Buy Pro." The purchase runs through the Microsoft Store (the same account you used to install StashWhisper), and your license is tied to that account, so it is restored automatically if you reinstall; see our Terms of Service and Refund Policy for how Store purchases and refunds work.
